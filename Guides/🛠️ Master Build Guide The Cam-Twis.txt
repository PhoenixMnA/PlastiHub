🛠️ Master Build Guide: The Cam-Twist Cable Lock
Materials Needed:
1 plastic bottle neck + matching cap

Utility knife or fine-tooth blade

Sandpaper or file (to smooth cut plastic)

Baseboard (wooden scrap or stiff fused sachet board)

Contact adhesive / hot glue

📐 Step-by-Step Construction
Carve the Bottle Neck Saddle (The Base):

Cut the bottle neck off the bottle.

On the top threaded rim of the bottle neck, carve two opposing downward U-shaped notches directly across from each other.

The Action: Your charging cable lays flat across the top of the bottle neck, resting snugly inside these two notches like a saddle!

Modify the Cap (The Cam Collar):

Cut out the top flat face of the cap so it becomes a threaded collar ring.

Remove a small section of the ring's outer wall so it has an open arc (making it easy to slip over threads or cables).

Carve or leave an inward-facing U-shaped tab/pocket on the inner rim of the cap wall.

Mount the Assembly:

Glue the bottom flange of your modified bottle neck flat onto your baseboard using strong adhesive and let it set completely.

The Lock in Action:

Lay your charging cord horizontally across the bottle mouth, seated inside the neck's downward U-notches.

Place your modified cap ring onto the threads.

As you screw the cap down, the inward U-shaped pocket on the cap wall rotates around and collides perpendicularly with the resting cord, wedging and clamping it tightly against the neck saddle! 🔒

The Multi-Cord Desk Rail (Power Station Edition):
Mount three Cam-Twist bottle necks side-by-side on a long fused-sachet plastic strip. This creates a full cable management rail that holds a phone charger, laptop cord, and earbud cable all in one organized station!
Glue a recycled magnet to the bottom of the baseboard. This allows students to snap the Cam-Twist cable manager directly onto the metal legs or sides of their study desks without using messy tapes or adhesives!
