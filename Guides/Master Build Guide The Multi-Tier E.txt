Master Build Guide: The Multi-Tier Eco-Caddy
Tools & Materials Needed:

3 empty plastic Coke bottles (standard 500ml or 1.5L size)

Utility knife or sharp scissors

Fine-grit sandpaper or a household clothes iron

Strong contact adhesive (like superglue, epoxy, or a hot glue gun)

Marker and a ruler

A sturdy baseboard (a piece of fused sachet plastic or stiff cardboard)

📐 Step-by-Step Construction
Step 1: Measure and Mark the Tiers

Wash and completely dry the 3 Coke bottles.

Using your ruler and marker, draw a straight line around the circumference of each bottle at three staggered heights. For a perfect cascading effect, measure them at 12 cm (for tall items like rulers and scissors), 8 cm (for pens and highlighters), and 5 cm (for erasers and flash drives).

Step 2: Cut and Smooth the Rims

Carefully pierce the plastic with your utility knife and cut along your marked lines. You will be using the bottom halves of the bottles.

The Pro Polish: Plastic edges can be sharp! To get a manufactured, professional feel, heat your clothes iron to a medium setting. Gently press the cut rim of each bottle flat against the warm iron for 1 to 2 seconds. The heat will cause the plastic to curl slightly inward, creating a smooth, rounded lip! (Alternatively, rub the edges vigorously with fine-grit sandpaper until completely smooth).

Step 3: The Cascading Alignment

Stand the three modified bottle cups upright on your desk.

Arrange them tightly side-by-side in a straight line or in a triangular cluster, descending from tallest to shortest.

Step 4: Bond the Structure

Apply a generous vertical strip of strong adhesive to the sides where the bottles touch each other. Press them together firmly and hold for 60 seconds until the glue sets.

Apply adhesive to the bottom rims of the entire cluster and press it firmly onto your baseboard. Once dry, use your utility knife to trim the excess baseboard flush with the bottle edges.

🎨 3 Creative Ways to Finish the Look!
To make this prototype absolutely shine during the presentation, we can add one final aesthetic touch. Here are three awesome ways to finish the caddy:

The "Sachet Sleeve" Wrap: Take a strip of your leftover waterproof fused sachet plastic and wrap it tightly around the entire outer circumference of the bottle cluster like a belt. It unifies this caddy perfectly with your desk tray!

The "Heavyweight Base" Upgrade: Before gluing the bottles together, pour a 1 cm layer of clean sand or melted, colorful bottle caps into the bottom of each cup. This gives the organizer a heavy, premium center of gravity so it never tips over!

The "Neon Edge" Trim: Slice very thin, colorful strips from spare bottle caps or old notebook covers, and superglue them around the smoothed top rims of the bottles for a vibrant, high-contrast pop of color.
