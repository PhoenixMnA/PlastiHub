Tools & Materials Needed:

12–16 clean water sachets, 2 sheets parchment paper, household iron, scissors/utility knife, ruler & marker, contact adhesive or needle/thread.

Step-by-Step Construction Process:

  1. Prep & Sanitize: Cut open seals of sachets, slice down one side to lay flat, wash thoroughly with soap/water, and dry completely.
  2. Layer the Stack: Place 1 sheet of parchment paper down, stack 6–8 sachet layers (alternating print directions for abstract patterns), and cover with 2nd parchment sheet.
  3. Thermal Fusion: Iron on medium/wool heat in slow circular motions for 10–15 seconds per side. Let cool 30 seconds and peel paper away to reveal waterproof plastic leather.
  4. Measure & Cut: Trim sheet to a 20cm x 15cm rectangle. Measure 3cm inward on all sides for wall fold lines, and cut 3cm slits at each corner.
  5. Fold, Bond & Compartments: Fold walls upward and glue or stitch overlapping corner tabs. Optional: Glue a 15cm x 3cm fused plastic strip across center as a divider wall.

Tools & Materials Needed:

1 plastic bottle neck + matching cap, utility knife/blade, sandpaper/file, mounting baseboard (wood/fused sachet board), contact adhesive.

Step-by-Step Construction Process:

  1. Carve the Saddle Base: Cut bottle neck off. Carve two opposing downward U-shaped notches across the top rim where charging cord rests.
  2. Modify the Cam Collar: Cut top face out of cap to form a ring, remove a small section for an open arc, and keep an inward U-shaped pocket on inner rim.
  3. Mount Assembly: Glue bottom flange of bottle neck flat onto baseboard.
  4. Locking Mechanism: Lay cord horizontally across neck saddle notches, thread modified cap ring on, and screw down—the inward U-pocket rotates and clamps cord perpendicularly against neck!
  5. Pro Upgrades: Mount 3 bottle necks side-by-side for a Multi-Cord Rail or glue a recycled magnet to base bottom for metal desk-leg snapping!

Tools & Materials Needed:

3 plastic Coke bottles, utility knife/scissors, spray paint & bowl of water (for dipping), iron/sandpaper, contact adhesive, ruler/marker, baseboard.

Step-by-Step Construction Process:

  1. Measure & Cut Tiers: Wash and dry 3 bottles. Mark circumference lines at staggered heights: 12cm (rulers/scissors), 8cm (pens), and 5cm (erasers). Cut bottom halves.
  2. Hydro Dip Custom Finish: Spray two colors into a bowl of water, swirl the pattern, gently dip each cut bottle half into the swirl, and let dry completely.
  3. Smooth Cut Rims: Press cut edges against a warm iron for 1–2 seconds to curl edges into a smooth rounded lip (or sand smooth).
  4. Bond Cascading Structure: Glue bottle sides together in a descending line or triangle. Glue cluster base onto baseboard and trim excess.
  5. Finish Touch: Wrap exterior with a fused sachet sleeve or pour 1cm of sand/caps into bottom cups for weighted stability.
